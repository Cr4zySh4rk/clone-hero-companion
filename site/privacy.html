Privacy

Clone Hero Companion works fully without an account. Signing in is optional, and everything here describes what happens only if you choose to.

Friends and sharing

Sharing is off by default. If you turn on sharing, all your friends can see your favourites and setlists (every setlist, including ones you create later); turn it off and no one can. Usernames are visible to people who search for them, together with your display name and picture; your email address is never shown to anyone.

If you sign in

You can sign in with Google or with a code sent to your email. When you are signed in, the app stores:

  • your email address, and your name and picture if you use Google;
  • your favourites;
  • your play history, taken from Clone Hero's scorestats.json: song, instrument, difficulty, score, stars, accuracy, notes hit, streak, time played and your computer's name;
  • an optional CH Leaderboards account link, if you link one: you sign in on CH Leaderboards' own page, our server checks it with CH and stores your CH account id, username and public profile. Your CH password and sign-in token are never stored.

The website

If you sign in on the Career page of this website, it reads only your own synced stats (your profile and your own play history) to show them to you. Nothing is written back, and nothing is sent anywhere else.

Where it lives

This data is kept in Supabase (hosted in the US) with row-level security, so each account can only read its own rows, except for your favourites and setlists if you turn on sharing with friends (see below). It is used only to sync your data between your machines. It is never sold or shared.

Other services the app talks to

To do its job the app contacts YouTube (through yt-dlp), lyrics providers, Chorus Encore, RhythmVerse and the public CH Leaderboards API. These requests send only search terms, such as a song and artist name.

Anonymous usage statistics

The app and this website count how they are used, so development can focus on what matters. It is anonymous.

App. Anonymous usage statistics are always on in the app (from 2.8.1 there is no setting to turn them off). You must accept the Terms of Use and this Privacy Policy on first launch (and again after material changes) before the app can be used; nothing is sent before you accept. What is sent:

  • A random install ID (a random UUID kept in the app's config folder, not derived from your hardware or account) and a random ID for each run of the app.
  • The app version, operating system family (windows, macos, linux), CPU architecture, and the app language.
  • At start: whether you are signed in (yes/no), a library size bucket (0, 1-50, 51-200, 201-1000, 1000+) and the kind of video acceleration (cpu, nvidia, apple, d3d11, vaapi).
  • Which screen is open (a fixed list such as library, charts, settings), about every 5 minutes while the app is open, and when you switch screens; and the length of the run in seconds when you quit.
  • Counts of actions, with at most a fixed choice attached: chart download (Encore or other), video added or failed, lyrics added or failed, video conversion (number bucket), Spotify import, setlist created, favourite on/off, stats shared (to which service), friend request sent, friend profile viewed, CH account link (ok or failed), app update (ok or failed), job cancelled, game launched.

Website. One page view per page load: the page path without any query, the domain you came from (if external), a screen-width bucket, and a random ID kept in your tab's sessionStorage (gone when the tab closes). Also clicks on download buttons (which system) and the Career sign-in method (Google or email). No cookies and no localStorage. Nothing is sent if your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control.

Never collected: song titles, artists, file paths, search text, usernames, names, email addresses or IP addresses (the server does not record them). Events can only carry the fixed properties above; the server rejects anything else.

Retention. Raw events are deleted after 180 days. Only the site owner can read the aggregated figures, through an admin-only dashboard; individual events are not readable by anyone through the app or website.

Deleting your data

To have your data deleted, open a GitHub issue or email adithyam211@gmail.com.

The current version of this policy is also kept in the project's repository.
