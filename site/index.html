Clone Hero Companion finds an HD video and synced lyrics for every song in your library, lines them up with the chart, and keeps doing it while you play. Free, open source, one download.

HD video, in sync

Select songs and press Run. The app finds the official music video, downloads it, and lines it up with the chart by matching the video's soundtrack against the chart audio. Only 640p or better is accepted, so no blurry uploads and no album-art slideshows.

  • Preparing starts the moment you highlight a song in Clone Hero
  • Per-song sync editor with live preview to nudge it by hand

Sing along

Charts that have a vocals part already show lyrics. For the rest, time-synced lyrics from LRCLIB are written into the chart and aligned to its own audio, so Clone Hero shows them in game.

Prefer to leave charts untouched? Switch lyrics off in one setting. Every change is backed up first and Undo restores the originals.

A real library manager

A virtualized list that stays fast with thousands of songs, with filters for no video, low-res video, no lyrics, favourites and recently added.

  • One-click metadata: album, year, genre, length and high-res art
  • Disable songs, rename folders by template, export chart packs
  • Find duplicate charts and keep the one you want
  • Create Clone Hero setlists from your library

Get charts

Search Chorus Encore and RhythmVerse with strict instrument and difficulty filters, preview the audio, and install with one click. New charts can get their video, lyrics and metadata right after they download.

  • .sng, .zip and .7z, including Google Drive, Mediafire and Dropbox links
  • Paste a Spotify playlist and see which songs you already have

Favourites that follow you

Star a song and it joins a Favourites setlist inside Clone Hero. Sign in, optionally, with Google or a six-digit code sent to your email, and your favourites sync across machines. A new computer can reinstall the lot in one click.

Signed out and offline still works fully.

Know your playing

After every song the app records the play. Profile shows plays, hours, accuracy, full combos and streaks, a 26-week activity map, your most played and best songs, and what needs practice, filterable by instrument and difficulty.

CH Leaderboards shows the global top scores right in the song panel and on Now Playing, and your rank once you link your username.

Install

Everything is bundled, ffmpeg and yt-dlp included.

  1. Open the .dmg and drag Clone Hero Companion to Applications.
  2. The app is ad-hoc signed, not notarized. On first launch, right-click it and choose Open, then Open again.
  3. If macOS asks about protected folders, grant Full Disk Access (Game & folders, then Grant Full Disk Access).
  1. Run the .exe.
  2. SmartScreen may warn because the app isn't code-signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. The window uses Microsoft Edge WebView2, which ships with Windows 10 and 11.
  1. Extract the archive: tar xzf CloneHeroCompanion-linux-x64.tar.gz
  2. Make it executable and run it: chmod +x CloneHeroCompanion && ./CloneHeroCompanion
  3. It opens in Chrome, Chromium or Edge app mode if installed, otherwise in your browser.

Then in Clone Hero, turn on Settings, Custom Content, Song Videos.
